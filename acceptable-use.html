Adult and Consent Rule

The core rule: every upload and prompt must involve adults only, and you must have the lawful right and required consent for the requested transformation.

You are responsible for your uploads, prompts, generated outputs, downloads, and distribution. The service may reject requests, suspend accounts, and preserve/report information when legally required.

Absolutely Prohibited

  • Anyone under 18, anyone described as under 18, or anyone who appears under 18.
  • Sexualization of minors, CSAM, age-play involving minors, or attempts to evade minor-safety rules.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, revenge porn, secretly captured intimate images, or coercive sexual content.
  • Sexual deepfakes of identifiable real people without the necessary authorization, including celebrities, public figures, classmates, coworkers, friends, ex-partners, or private individuals.
  • Trafficking, sexual exploitation, coercion, blackmail, extortion, stalking, harassment, or threats.
  • Incest involving minors, bestiality, extreme illegal sexual material, or illegal content generally.
  • Impersonation intended to harm, deceive, exploit, or damage reputation.
  • Content created for illegal distribution or to violate someone else's privacy, copyright, publicity, or image rights.
  • Uploads you do not have the legal right to use.

Service Enforcement

We may reject prompts or generations, remove access, suspend or terminate accounts, revoke promotional credits, preserve information, review reports, and cooperate with lawful requests. We may use technical filters and operational review, but we do not guarantee detection of every violation.

Reporting Violations

If you believe content involves minors, non-consensual sexual content, copyright infringement, privacy violations, or other abuse, report it at Report Abuse or email support@undressgoon.app.
