High-Priority Reports

Minors or non-consensual sexual content: report immediately. Email undressgoon@proton.me with URGENT in the subject if a minor, apparent minor, or non-consensual intimate image may be involved.

Include only information needed to locate and review the issue. Do not send unnecessary sensitive images unless needed to identify the content.

Report Form

This static cPanel site does not currently have a server-side report form. The form below opens your email app with the report details. If it does not open, email undressgoon@proton.me directly.

What to Include

  • Your contact email.
  • The category of report.
  • URL, job/content identifier, account email, Telegram username/user ID, payment/order ID, or approximate date if known.
  • A concise explanation of the issue.
  • For depicted-person removal requests, explain your connection to the depicted person or confirm that you are the depicted person.
