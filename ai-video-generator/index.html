How to get a more coherent AI video

Video generation has to preserve a subject while also creating new motion across many frames. A simple source image and focused action generally produce a more stable result.

For a stronger image-to-video result

  • keep one adult subject clear and large in the frame;
  • use even lighting and avoid heavy blur or filters;
  • choose one principal action and a stable finishing pose;
  • avoid asking for several camera moves, outfit changes and body positions at once;
  • keep the requested movement compatible with the visible starting pose.
