Refund & Credit Policy

Credits are usage rights for generation requests. This page explains what is and is not refundable.

Credits

Credits let you request generations inside UndressGoon. Credits are not cash, stored value, or a transferable currency. Pack sizes and prices are shown before purchase.

The current website normally spends one credit per generated image request, unless a promotion, referral, pack, or service setting changes that amount.

Free and Promotional Credits

Free credits are offered so users can test the service before buying. Promotional and referral credits may be limited, changed, revoked for abuse, or discontinued. They have no cash value.

Failed Generations

When a website generation starts, the backend reserves the required credit. If the job fails because the service cannot complete it, the current backend is designed to refund the reserved credit automatically.

If a job succeeds but you dislike the quality, style, pose, anatomy, prompt interpretation, or creative result, that is not a failed generation and is not refundable.

Refunds

Except where required by law, completed credit purchases and spent credits are final and non-refundable. Free credits exist so you can assess quality before purchasing.

We may investigate duplicate charges, unauthorized purchases, or fulfillment errors. Contact support with the order ID, payment provider, date, amount, account email, and a short explanation.

Crypto Payments

Crypto checkout is processed by a third-party provider. Crypto payments can be irreversible and may depend on blockchain confirmation and provider status. Sending the wrong coin, chain, amount, or wallet details may be impossible to recover.

Chargebacks and Abuse

Fraudulent disputes, abusive chargebacks, or attempts to use credits without payment may lead to account suspension, loss of promotional credits, and preservation of records needed for dispute response.
