No. You only add a payment method if you decide to buy credits later. Packs can be paid by card or crypto.

Yes, a quick Google or email-code sign-in. It's what keeps your credits and results attached to you.

How long does a generation take?

Usually 60 seconds. Failed generations are refunded automatically.

What images can I upload?

JPG, PNG or WebP up to 12 MB. A clear, face-forward photo gives the best results. Only upload photos of adults you have the rights and consent to edit, see our Acceptable Use and Consent Policy.

How do credits work?

1 credit = 1 generated image. You buy them in one-time packs, and bigger packs cost less per image.

No. Credit packs are one-time purchases. Nothing renews, nothing charges you again.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits stay on your account until you use them.

Are my generations public?

No, there is no public gallery. Your generations appear only inside your own account session, never posted anywhere public.

What if a generation fails?

The credit is refunded to your balance automatically and you can try again.
