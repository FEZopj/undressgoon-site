18+ Only

You must be at least 18 years old to use the service. You must also be legally allowed to access and create adult content in your location.

Do not upload, request, describe, or attempt to create sexual content involving minors or anyone who appears under 18.

Generation Consent Checkbox

The generator requires a consent acknowledgement. The checkbox must not be pre-checked. The current backend also requires the acknowledgement before accepting a web generation request.

The acknowledgement is concise on purpose: you confirm that you are 18+ and have the necessary rights and consent to use the image.

Safer Use

  • Use fictional AI-generated adults or consenting adults only.
  • Do not upload private images of others without consent.
  • Do not distribute outputs in a way that violates privacy, consent, copyright, publicity rights, platform rules, or law.
  • Report suspected minors, non-consensual content, harassment, or copyright issues promptly.
