Before you generate

You must be 18+ and have the subject's explicit consent and the necessary image rights. The tool is never permitted for minors or non-consensual content.

Better inputs save credits

Pose, lighting, framing and obstruction all affect the generated result. Check the source image first.

Even lighting

Avoid deep shadows, colored club lighting and blown-out highlights where possible.

Clear framing

Keep one adult subject large enough in the frame, with body contours visible.

Simple pose

Front-facing or simple three-quarter poses usually generate more coherently.
