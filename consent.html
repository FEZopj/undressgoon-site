What You May Upload

You may upload an image only when you have the lawful right to use it for the requested AI transformation. If another identifiable adult is depicted, you must have the appropriate authorization and consent for the specific adult edit you request.

Images should be used for lawful personal fantasy, fictional AI-generated adults, or consenting adults only.

What You May Not Upload

  • Minors under any circumstance, including people who appear under 18.
  • Secretly taken intimate images or private images shared without permission.
  • Ex-partner revenge content or content intended to shame, harass, threaten, or extort.
  • Celebrities, public figures, influencers, classmates, coworkers, friends, or strangers for sexual transformation without authorization.
  • Images copied from social media, dating apps, private chats, cloud backups, school/work accounts, or someone else's device unless you have the necessary rights and consent.

Your Responsibility

By submitting an image, you confirm that you are 18+ and that you have the necessary rights and consent. You also confirm the requested output will not violate law, privacy rights, publicity rights, copyright, or the Acceptable Use Policy.

Removal Requests from Depicted Persons

If you are depicted in content connected to the service and want to request removal or review, use the Report Abuse page or email undressgoon@proton.me. Include enough detail to identify the content, such as URL, job/content identifier, approximate date, account details if known, and a brief explanation.
